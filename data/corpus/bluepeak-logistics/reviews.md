# Customer reviews summary: Bluepeak Logistics

> Fictional company and documents, created for the diligence-agents benchmark. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.

Across 212 customer reviews, Bluepeak Logistics averages 4.3 out of 5. Customers praise tracking and communication. The most common complaint, in about 8 percent of reviews, is late delivery during the December peak season. Several customers note that the company issued service credits for late deliveries.

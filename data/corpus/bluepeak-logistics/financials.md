# Bluepeak Logistics: financial summary FY2025

> Fictional company and documents, created for the diligence-agents benchmark. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.

Bluepeak Logistics reported revenue of 84 million dollars in fiscal 2025, up 9 percent from 77 million in fiscal 2024. Operating margin was 7.5 percent. The company has a revolving credit line of 10 million dollars, of which 2 million was drawn at year end. Its auditor issued an unqualified opinion. Days sales outstanding improved from 48 to 41 days.

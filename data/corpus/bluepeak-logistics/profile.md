# Bluepeak Logistics Ltd: company profile

> Fictional company and documents, created for the diligence-agents benchmark. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.

Bluepeak Logistics Ltd is a freight and courier company founded in 2009 and headquartered in Columbus, Ohio. It operates 14 regional depots and employs about 620 people. Its main services are pallet freight, same-day courier, and warehouse storage for mid-size retailers and healthcare suppliers. The company holds ISO 9001 certification, renewed in 2025.

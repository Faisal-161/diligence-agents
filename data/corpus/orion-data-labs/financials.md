# Orion Data Labs: financial summary FY2025

> Fictional company and documents, created for the diligence-agents benchmark. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.

Orion Data Labs reported revenue of 21 million dollars in fiscal 2025, down 40 percent from 35 million in fiscal 2024, after its largest customer moved labeling in-house. The company posted a net loss of 6 million dollars. Its auditor included a going-concern paragraph, citing cash on hand of about 5 months of operating expenses.

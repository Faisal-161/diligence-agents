# Orion Data Labs Inc.: company profile

> Fictional company and documents, created for the diligence-agents benchmark. Any resemblance to real companies is coincidental.

Orion Data Labs Inc. provides data labeling and annotation services for machine learning teams. It was founded in 2018 in Boston and grew quickly during 2021 to 2023. It currently employs about 260 people, down from 410 a year earlier.
